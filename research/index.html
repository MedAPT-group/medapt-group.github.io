Research Introduction
MedAPT (Medical AI for Precision Therapy) develops medical AI methods that connect data-driven generative models with physical principles to solve difficult medical inverse problems. Our goal is to make precision cancer care—especially proton therapy—more accurate, efficient, and accessible.
Research Framework

Our work follows a connected path from clinical applications, through core imaging tasks, to algorithms that make the pathway possible.

Applications

Clinical settings that motivate the research.

  • Cancer Early Screening
  • AI-driven Treatment Planning Systems
  • Image-Guided Radiotherapy
  • Upright Proton Therapy

Tasks

Medical imaging capabilities that support precision therapy.

  • Image Reconstruction
  • Image Generation
  • Image Registration
  • Image Segmentation
  • 4D Motion Analysis

Algorithms

Learning and physical modeling tools that connect data to care.

  • Physics-Informed Neural Networks
  • 3D Gaussian Splatting
  • Flow-Matching Generative Models
  • Foundational Medical Models
